## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Open container on the inventory pillar (Board, 2026-11-14): with a log basket / forestry basket or fish barrel carried open and its contents known, the inventory side pillar is one bar out of the container's capacity plus 28: the container fills first at the bottom in its own colour, the inventory on top in the usual green-to-red, one mark per slot, and the number is the free total (`55` for an empty basket and 27 free slots; used total with Inventory count = Used). The tooltip lists the container first, then the inventory. Closed, unchecked, coal bag or the setting off: the inventory-only bar exactly as before (`InventoryPillar`). New keys `pillarContainers` (`Containers on the pillar`, default on) and `pillarContainerColour` (`Container colour`, default violet). Essence pouches have no open state and are not merged.
- Blast Furnace pump badge (Board, 2026-11-14), the first training method (`TrainingMethod`, a table more methods slot into). Clicking `Operate` on the pump (gameval `ObjectID.BLAST_FURNACE_PUMP`, 9090) and animating beside it raises the skilling badge as `Pumping (Blast Furnace) | Strength 70` with XP to next, session XP, XP/h and time to level like any skill, in every badge position (over your head, over the pump, fixed). The inventory line becomes `Re-click in 4:12 | Stops in 97:30`: the idle logout from RuneLite's own idle counters (`min(keyboard, mouse idle)` against the client's idle timeout, 5 to 25 minutes), and the pump's own stop 100 minutes after the click ([OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pump_(Blast_Furnace))). Stopping gives `Not pumping` and the usual idle timer. Three new alerts, all with sound, volume and repeat like the others and default on: `Stopped pumping` (no animation or Strength XP for the stopped delay; not on walking away or a fight), `Idle logout soon` and `Pump stops soon`, each `alertMethodWarnSeconds` (default 60 s) ahead (`TrainingSession`). Voice lines "You've stopped pumping.", "Click soon, or you'll be logged out.", "The pump stops soon. Click it again." New keys `alertMethodStopped`, `alertReclickSoon`, `alertAutoStopSoon`, `alertMethodWarnSeconds` and `sound`/`volume`/`repeat` + `MethodStopped`/`ReclickSoon`/`AutoStopSoon`.
- Sidebar panel (Board, 2026-11-14: "so many settings it is daunting"): the plugin's own button in RuneLite's sidebar, with every setting from RuneLite's list (same keys, same values; a change in either shows in the other) laid out as presets and five tabs: Combat, Targets, Skilling, Alerts, Look (`VitalsSidePanel`, `SettingControls`, `PanelLayout`). Each tab opens with its own short guide: what it does, how to use it, and a walkthrough (`PanelGuide`; the same pages in README "Sidebar panel"). Inputs are built from RuneLite's `ConfigDescriptor`, so names, tooltips and ranges match the list; writes go through `ConfigManager` off the Swing thread. Patterns from GIM Utilities' panel (CardLayout tabs, `Column`, headings, folding sections). No reflection.
- Presets: Skiller, PvM, Minimal, Everything. Clicking one previews exactly which settings change, from what to what, before Apply; a preset only writes the existing keys it lists (`Presets`), and Undo puts each back as it was, an unset key included.
- Alerts tab: one row per alert with its on/off switch, its own sound, volume and Repeat, and a Test button that plays it as set up now (even while switched off; never repeats).
- Same for every alert (Board, 2026-11-14): new additive keys `alertSoundShared` (`Same for every alert`, default off), `alertSoundAll`, `alertVolumeAll` (40), `alertRepeatAll`. On, one sound, volume and repeat apply to every alert, sound packs included; each alert's own keys are kept untouched for when it is off, and on/off stays per alert (`AlertSettings.of`).
- Look tab preview: the health colours as a bar fading full to empty, and where the skilling badge sits for its position, offset and size (`LookPreview`).
- The skilling badge for Herblore (Board, 2026-11-14: "the skilling one doesn't show when I'm doing herblore"), Cooking, Fletching, Crafting, Smithing, Firemaking, Prayer, Magic (skilling spells, never combat ones), Agility, Construction and Sailing. Raised by the skill's XP drop, held by further drops or any animation, then the usual linger and "Not doing herblore" idle badge (`XpSkilling`). Never during a fight, on a tick with Hitpoints XP, or over a fishing/tree/rock badge. Level, XP to next, session count ("14 made", "27 burned", "5 cast"), rates and time to level as for the other skills. The inventory line says what is left to process, learned from what each action used up: "14 left, done in ~25s" (`ProductionInputs`); for Agility it reads laps, last lap time and marks of grace (`AgilityLaps`). New per-skill switches `skillingHerblore` … `skillingSailing`, default on. Farming is not covered.
- Two master switches at the top of the settings (Board, 2026-11-14: one plugin, not a split): `combatVitals` (`Combat vitals`) and `skillingVitals` (`Skilling vitals`), new additive keys, both default on so nothing changes for anyone. Off takes that half's overlays, side pillars and alerts away entirely and leaves the other half as set (`VitalsHalves`). Combat: the panel, the readout over the target and over you, the game health bar swap, the combat pillar. Skilling: the badge, the skilling pillars, the next fishing spot mark and path, activity and boost alerts (a repeating alert stops). `ConfigKeyFreezeTest` pins the config group and every key and type against `published-config-keys.txt`.
- Skilling containers (Board, 2026-11-14): under the badge's inventory line, one line per container carried or worn that holds what the current skill gathers: log basket / forestry basket while woodcutting, fish barrel / fish sack barrel while fishing, coal bag while mining (27, 36 with a worn Smithing cape), essence pouches while runecrafting: `Log basket 12/28 free`. Basket, barrel and coal bag contents come from the game's chat lines (Check, emptied, full, one more per catch while open) and from the inventory change after a Fill or Empty click; never guessed, so a container not checked since login reads `Log basket ?/28 (check the basket once)`. Pouches read their varbits, capacity from the base client's `EssencePouchOverlay` table. New `skillingContainers` (`Show containers`, default on) and `skillingContainersAsInventory` (`Count containers as free`, default off): on, the inventory line reads `Inv 27/28 free (+16 basket)` and Inventory full waits for the container too; the coal bag (coal only) and unknown contents never count. The inventory side pillar's tooltip lists the same lines. Log baskets, barrels, coal bags and pouches now hold their slot instead of counting as filling (`SkillingContainer`, `ContainerContents`, `ContainerRow`, `EssencePouch`). Gem bag, herb sack, seed box and plank sack are not covered; see BRIEF.md.
- Alert sounds (Board, 2026-11-14), new closed `Alert sounds` section, all keys new and additive. Per activity alert: a sound (`sound<Alert>`: RuneLite / Soft chime / Music box / Retro blip / Voice line / My sounds, default RuneLite, which is exactly the old behaviour), a volume for the plugin's own sounds (`volume<Alert>`, 1-100, default 40), and `Repeat` (`repeat<Alert>`, default off). With a sound of the plugin's own, RuneLite's notification still shows (tray, flash, focus as set up) with its own sound turned off for that alert (`AlertSettings.quiet`); the plugin plays through RuneLite's `AudioPlayer`.
- Repeat until you're back: an alert with `Repeat` on sounds again every `repeatEverySeconds` (default 15 s) until a mouse press or key press in the client, experience again in a gathering, thieving or make-X skill, a logout or hop, or `repeatMaxMinutes` (default 10). Soft and never louder over time. The plugin only listens to input; it never sends any (`AlertRepeat`).
- Voice lines: "You've stopped" + the activity + what you were gathering ("You've stopped chopping yew logs."), joined in memory from short clips listed in `voice/lines.tsv`; an unknown item says "You've stopped chopping", an unknown activity "You've stopped gathering." (`VoiceLines`). The clips are rendered offline by `tools/voice/generate_voice.py` (Kokoro-82M, Apache-2.0; see NOTICE). Until they are bundled, Voice line falls back to RuneLite's sound.
- My sounds: your own WAV per alert at `~/.runelite/combat-vitals/sounds/<alert>.wav` (for example `gathering-stopped.wav`). A missing file plays RuneLite's sound and says so in the chat once.
- Sound packs Soft chime, Music box and Retro blip, synthesised by `tools/sounds/generate_packs.py`: the project's own, no third-party audio (117 KB).

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-06

The first update since the Plugin Hub release (`7e9eac9`, 2026-09-02): PRs #2 to #21 and the fixes from the Board's in-game test of 2026-11-04. In short: a skilling badge for fishing, woodcutting, mining, thieving, hunter and runecraft (level, boost timer, XP, session rates, inventory fill, catchable fish, missing-gear warning, idle state); activity alerts; side pillars; a path to the next fishing spot through Shortest Path; separate display for bosses; overhead chat kept readable above the readout; and an opt-in option to hide the game's own health bar. Pinned to RuneLite 1.13.6, the Hub's `runelite.version`.

### Added
- Update notes in chat (Board, 2026-11-09): on the first login after the plugin updates, a few game-chat lines say what is new, once per version; a fresh install gets one welcome line instead. The last version handled is stored in a hidden `lastSeenVersion` key, written even with the new `showUpdateNotes` (`Show update notes in chat`, Advanced, default on) turned off. 1.0.0 stored no version, so nothing stored reads as an update from 1.0.0 when the plugin starts on the login screen (as Hub updates do) and as a fresh install when it starts logged in (as installs from the Plugin Hub panel usually do). The version lives in `UpdateNotes.VERSION`, held equal to `runelite-plugin.properties` by `UpdateNotesTest`.
- `Hide game health bar` draws back every bar it hides (Board's in-game test, 2026-11-06, which rejected hiding bars it did not redraw): every player and NPC the game reports a bar for (`getHealthRatio` / `getHealthScale`) that is not your target or you gets a plain green and red bar in the game's own place, its width the bar's scale, its fill the ratio, lifted 12 px while the actor talks, as the client's `drawActor2d` does (`GameHealthBars`, `GameHealthBarOverlay`, `GameHealthBarsTest` with a crowd).
- Next fishing spot mark (Board's in-game test, 2026-11-06: Shortest Path's line showed for a moment and went). Shortest Path ends a path once you are within its `finishDistance` (default 5 tiles), read from its own config, which a `PluginMessage` cannot change; the next spot is usually closer than that. The spot is now marked by the plugin itself until you fish again: tile outline, a line from you to it, and an arrow at the edge of the game view while it is off screen (`NextSpotOverlay`, `EdgeArrow`). New `fishingSpotShortestPath` (`Next spot: Shortest Path`, default on) makes Shortest Path optional.
- While pickpocketing, your own health shows above the skilling badge for as long as you are at it (Board, 2026-11-06).
- `Badge layout` (`skillingBadgeLayout`, Full / Compact, default Full). Compact is one short session line, `24 caught | 10.6k xp/h` (`SessionRow.compactCells`, `BadgeLayout`).
- Separate display for bosses and high-HP NPCs (group member feedback via the Board, 2026-11-04: percent on bosses, hitpoints on everything else). New Targets settings `bigTargetDisplay` (Big targets) and `otherTargetDisplay` (Other targets), each Same as Display / Hitpoints / Percentage / Both, default Same as Display so a profile that never sets them looks as before; `bigTargetRule` (Big target is: Bosses only / Bosses + max HP / Max HP only, default Bosses + max HP) and `bigTargetMaxHp` (200, 1-10000). A boss is an NPC the game's boss health HUD is showing (`HPBAR_HUD_NPC`) or one on a bundled list from the OSRS wiki's Boss page, read 2026-11-04. Max HP is NPCManager's; unknown counts as an other target. Players and your own health keep Display (`BigTargets`, `TargetDisplay`, `BigTargetRule`, #21).
- Side pillars that say what they are (Board's in-game test, 2026-11-04): each carries an icon at its top (the skill's, a backpack, the hitpoints heart) and a number under it (XP percent or XP left, free or used slots, the target's hitpoints), the XP pillar its level at the foot, and a hover text, `Fishing XP: 82% to level 17 (65 xp left)`, `Inventory: 10 of 28 free`, `Target: Rock Crab 12/50`. Drawn like RuneLite's Status Bars (BSD-2, `BarRenderer`'s background, inset and offsets reimplemented): a dark frame, a lit fill with a shine, a mark every 25% on XP and per slot on the inventory, a glide to each new value, dimmed while idle. New `pillarLabels` (Icon and number / Icon only / None) and `pillarStyle` (Status Bars / Flat) (`PillarPainter`, `PillarText`, `PillarAnimation`, `PillarIcons`, #19).
- Session row redesign: a faint rule between columns, the skill icon before the count, a soft shade of the skill colour per stat, short numbers and the action's noun, a session timer: `24 caught in 3m | 10.6k xp/h | 459 fish/h`. New `skillingTimeToLevel` (default on, shown with the rates): `lvl 18 in 6m` (`SessionRow`, #19).
- Path to next fishing spot (Board, 2026-11-04), new `fishingSpotPath` setting, default `Off`. `On spot move`: when the spot you are fishing moves, the nearest active spot of the same kind (net/bait, lure/bait, cage/harpoon, big net/harpoon, barbarian) that the method you used can catch something at is sent to the Shortest Path plugin; `Right-click only`, and `On spot move` too, add `Path to next spot` when right-clicking the fishing badge. Nothing of that kind loaded: the nearest bundled location (`fishing_spots.tsv`, from the OSRS wiki, filtered by members world and the location's own level, e.g. the Fishing Guild's 68). The path clears when you start fishing again; a spot that moves twice in a tick sends one path, and a target that moves before you get there is searched for again. Without Shortest Path, the next loaded spot's tile is outlined instead. Talks to Shortest Path only through RuneLite's `PluginMessage` (`ShortestPathBridge`, copied from QuestPath). Pure, unit-tested `NextFishingSpot`, `SpotPathing`, `KnownFishingSpots`, `ShortestPathBridge` (#16, #20).
- Skilling badge on objects: chopping a tree, mining a rock, stealing from a stall or crafting runes at an altar shows the same badge (skill icon, level, boost, timer, tool spec energy). Objects never fire `InteractingChanged`, so the badge needs both the clicked option (`Chop down`, `Chop`, `Mine`, `Steal-from`, `Craft-rune`) and the player animating within reach of the object; it lingers for the timeout when the animation stops in place, and clears at once on walking away, `Walk here`, a non-skilling object option, or engaging any NPC. Attacking still wins. Pure, unit-tested `ObjectSkilling` (#12).
- Inventory fill bar under the skilling badge, green through yellow to red by the share of free capacity filled; stackables, noted items, tools and wieldable or wearable items hold their slot and do not count (`InventoryFill`, `skillingInventoryBar`, #11).
- Activity alerts, a new config section of RuneLite notifications, each with its own toggle: inventory full while gathering or thieving, stopped gathering (no animation or experience for `alertStopSeconds`, default 5; never on walking off, banking, dropping or depositing, logout or hop), fishing spot moved, stopped thieving (stuns excluded), pickpocket stun (off), coin pouches at cap (`alertCoinPouchCap`, default 28), dodgy necklace broke, make-X finished for cooking, fletching, crafting and smithing (off), and low run energy (off, `alertRunEnergyThreshold` default 10). Pure, unit-tested `ActivityAlerts` state machine (#11).
- Skilling badge v2: the badge is drawn over your own head, at the same anchor as your own health bar; when both are up it stacks above the health bar (`OverheadStack`). On a fishing spot it shows the fish the clicked method (Net, Bait, Lure, Cage, Harpoon, Big Net, Use-rod, Fish) can land at your current boosted level, as small item icons, with the last catch (from the `You catch ...` message or the inventory) leading. Spot type from the base client's `FishingSpot` NPC id table; levels and methods from the OSRS wiki, in `FishingCatches`; unknown spots or methods fall back to the skill and level. New `skillingCatches` toggle. Pure, unit-tested `FishingCatches`, `InventoryCatches`, `OverheadStack` (#9).
- Skilling vitals badge: working a fishing spot or another non-attackable skilling NPC draws a badge in the health bar's style, with the skill's icon, your boosted level and boost (`Fishing 79 (+3)`), the seconds until the boost drops by one (Preserve-aware, marked `~` until an observed drop confirms the drain phase), the remaining special attack energy when a dragon/infernal/crystal/3rd age axe, pickaxe or harpoon is wielded, and the boost's source when it was just drunk, eaten or spec'd. A drop or expiry paints the badge red for a moment, with an optional RuneLite notification (off by default). New `Skilling vitals` config section (`skillingEnabled`, `skillingBoostTimer`, `skillingBoostSource`, `skillingSpecEnergy`, `skillingBoostNotify`). Pure, unit-tested `SkillingActions`, `SkillingTools`, `BoostTracker`, `BoostTimer`, `SkillingState` and `SkillingText` (#7).
- `Hide game health bar` option, reinstated by the Board after the `7e9eac9` cut, now built on transparent sprite overrides for the 22 standard bar sprites rather than the 2D draw hook: global (yours included), reasserted each client tick so Interface Styles' HD bars are handed back rather than cleared, restored on toggle-off and shutdown (`NativeHealthBarHider`, `NativeHealthBarHiderTest`, `docs/native-hp-bar.md`, `5f65626`, `7fcb8e1`, #10).
- `Chat above readout` option (`hideGameOverheadChat`): while the overlay redraws an actor's overhead chat above the readout, the game's own line is blanked the way the base client's Chat Filter blanks a message, countdown untouched, and handed back when the readout moves clear, the option is off, or the plugin shuts down (`OverheadChatHolder`, `OverheadChatHolderTest`, `docs/overhead-chat.md`, `0aad7b8`, #10).

### Fixed
- Thieving: a stun or a hit from the NPC being pickpocketed counted as combat and hid the badge with `Hide during combat`. A hit on you while thieving is now combat only when an attackable NPC or another player is interacting with you (a stall's guard, say) (`ThievingHits`).
- Slow `Not fishing` (Board's in-game test, 2026-11-06): after walking away the badge waited out the whole linger, the 5 s timeout, about 8 ticks. It now switches the first tick the player's tile changes after the interaction ends, or, for fishing, after 2 ticks standing still off the spot and not animating; fishing through animation gaps never trips it, and a click on the next spot counts as working until its interaction arrives (`SkillingStop`).
- Catch icons ran into the word `free` in the inventory line at some counts: the label's space is reserved at the font's widest digit with a gap, and the icons are drawn from the right edge (`InventoryLine`).
- The Thieving icon vanished on the session row's dark background, so the row seemed to have none: every skill's session icon now sits on the same subtle light backing, so the look is consistent and nothing depends on a brightness threshold (`SkillingBadgeRenderer.drawSessionIcon`, `SessionIconTest`).
- `Next spot path: On spot move` never fired (Board's in-game test, 2026-11-04: the log showed only right-click paths). A fishing spot moves by relocating: the same NPC shows on a new tile, with no despawn or id change, as RuneLite's own FishingPlugin assumes when it resets a minnow spot on a world location change. The pather now reads the fished spot's tile each tick and a change, while you are on it or within 2 ticks of the interaction ending, is the spot moving; walking or clicking away first still never paths. The spot being walked to relocating searches again. Despawn and `NpcChanged` still count (`SpotPathing.Locator`).
- Lag spikes: with `Hide game health bar` on and a bar sprite not yet readable, every client tick decoded every missing sprite from the game cache again (22 reads a frame); missing sprites are now retried about once a second (`NativeHealthBarHider.RETRY_CALLS`). Measured in `FramePathBenchmarkTest`.
- `Error during overlay rendering` from `getCanvasTextLocation` just after a kill, while the dead NPC was being removed: the opponent and its readout are dropped when it despawns, and a client exception from any on-screen lookup skips that actor for the frame instead of failing the overlay (`CombatVitalsOverheadOverlay.onScreen`, `OverheadOnScreenTest`).
- Overhead chat drawn twice (Board's in-game test, 2026-11-04). With `Chat above readout` off the plugin no longer draws its own copy, so the game's line is the only one. With it on, a new message over you or your opponent is blanked inside `OverheadTextChanged`, before any frame is drawn, so the game's copy no longer flashes under the readout (`OverheadChatHolder.hold`, `heldText`, `OverheadChat.baseline`, #14).
- Right-click black screen: `FishingSpotPath` must be public for the config panel (#18).
- Pickpocketing an attackable NPC (a Man) now shows the Thieving badge: the clicked option decides before attackability, and a hit you land on it still makes it a fight (#12).
- The boost source for a Drink or Eat is labelled `likely` (`likely Fishing potion`), since any item consumed within three ticks of a rise is named (#12).
- The overhead readout no longer paints over the target's overhead chat: while an actor's chat is showing and the readout covers the game's line, the line is drawn just above the readout in the game's bold font. Only actors this plugin draws over qualify: the opponent and you, with one copy of your chat lifted above the whole stack (`OverheadStack.union`). A skilling NPC, or any bystander, is never touched (`OverheadChat`, `OverheadChatTest`, `88b5d70`, `1358522`, #2, #10).
- Clicking a skilling NPC (fishing spot, rock, banker, shopkeeper, pet) no longer pops your own health bar or holds a stale opponent: a target counts only if it is attackable (`Attack` menu option or a combat level), your own bar also shows for `opponentTimeout` after any hitsplat on you or dealt by you, and the opponent expires unless you are still interacting with that same actor (`CombatState`, `CombatStateTest`, #4).
